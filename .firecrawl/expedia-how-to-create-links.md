# How do I create Affiliate Links?


Updated 9 months ago

Of all the tools available, affiliate **links** (also known as tracking links) are the bread and butter of any affiliate business. Our partners love links in the [Expedia Group Travel Creator Program](https://creator.expediagroup.com/) because they are easy to create and flexible to use on different platforms, including websites, blogs, social media and apps.

If you're new to affiliate marketing, this guide will walk you through the necessary steps to create your first links using our tools. When choosing your desired page on [any Expedia Group brand site](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/12480156695191), you have 2 convenient routes to create links:

1\. The new **Creator Toolbox**, which lets you build links without ever leaving our sites

2\. Our standard link builder

Read on to learn about both options.

[Creator Toolbox](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/13164069784727-How-do-I-create-Affiliate-Links#tab-content-40) [Link builder](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/13164069784727-How-do-I-create-Affiliate-Links#tab-content-41)

### How to activate the Creator Toolbox

Once you’ve signed in, navigate to the [Creator Toolbox](https://console.vap.expedia.com/eg-affiliates/products/creator-toolbox?utm_source=Help_Center&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=learn_about_the_creator_toolbox) page. Select a site from the dropdown menu and click “Open Creator Toolbox” to start browsing this site. The site should look completely normal but with a navy bar present on top.

![Screenshot 2025-07-16 at 4.00.52 pm.png](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/33508038318999)

### How to create and copy an affiliate link

When you're on any Expedia site page, you'll able to generate a link via the Creator Toolbox instantly. Just hit the **Get link** button in the top right corner.

![Screenshot 2025-07-16 at 4.02.14 pm.png](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/33508043707671)

Once you hit this button, your tracked affiliate link will be automatically copied into your clipboard – no need to copy it manually. From there, simply paste it wherever you desire!

### How can I see specific info on properties?

On certain pages, such as hotels, the Creator Toolbox is able to show stats that may be helpful in creating content that earns. When stats are available, you'll see this option in the Creator Toolbox:

![Screenshot 2025-07-16 at 4.02.59 pm.png](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/33508043709591)

We currently provide stats related to commissions, [cookie durations](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/13164005434519), ratings, reviews and related bookings.

[Learn more about the Creator Toolbox](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/15782595650711).

### Sign in to the Travel Creator Dashboard

As tracked affiliate links need to be unique to you, they can only be created once you're logged in. Click [here](https://console.vap.expedia.com/eg-affiliates/login) to log in. If you're struggling to log in, try [resetting your password](https://console.vap.expedia.com/eg-affiliates-auth/forgot-password) or [contact us](mailto:creatorsupport@expediagroup.com) for additional support.

### Visit the [Links](https://console.vap.expedia.com/eg-affiliates/products/links) page

Once logged in, you're greeted with the main Travel Creator Dashboard that shows you all the tools and information you need to manage your affiliate business at a glance. To get started, visit the _Links_ page either via the button or the side navigation, as shown below.

![](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/13807740609047)

_Access the Link page to begin creating your affiliate links._

### Choose between: a) creating your own links or b) copying a quick link

On the Links page, you have the option to either create a custom affiliate link or copy a pre-loaded quick link. To summarize the differences:

- **a) Create an affiliate link** when you know exactly which page on an Expedia Group site you wish to direct your audience to. There are many different types of pages you can link to on our sites, so feel free to [read our guide on the types of pages available](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/12480156695191-Types-of-pages-you-can-link-to) to learn the differences.
- **b) Quick links** are pre-populated based on the brand site chosen via the _Point of sale_ selector. These are our most popular pages on the chosen website and are great if you need some inspiration for where to start.

### a) How to create and copy a custom affiliate link

To create a custom link, choose a desired page on any Expedia Group brand site. Then, copy its URL and paste it into the link creation tool. Once you press "Create link", a tracked affiliate link is generated. You can also shorten the link by clicking on "Shorten links".

![](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/13823014879639)

_Above, we've demonstrated the link creation process with a hotel listed on Expedia.com. This process works with any page, including home pages, search results pages, and more._

### b) How to select and copy a quick link

Quick links vary depending on the _point of sale_ selected. You can change your point of sale with the dropdown selector, then scroll down for a list of the most popular pages. Once you find the link you'd like to use, click the "Copy link" button so that it's instantly added to your clipboard.

![](https://help.creator.expediagroup.com/hc/article_attachments/13822699047703)

_The 'Point of sale' selector shows all Expedia Group brand site and country options available in the program. The above illustrates how easy it is to populate quick links to Hotels.com in the USA._

From there, you're done! You can use these links wherever you own and post content. This can range from a website to a blog, to social media.
