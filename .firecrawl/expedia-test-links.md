# How do I test my Affiliate Links?


Updated 9 months ago

After creating your first link, it's best practice to test it. This ensures that it's tracking properly, which is essential to earning commission on any bookings made through your links. To do this, we recommend you follow these steps:

1. Create a tracked affiliate link in the Travel Creator Dashboard.
2. Open a web browser and paste your affiliate link in the address bar.
3. See your performance in the [Travel Creator Dashboard](http://creator.expediagroup.com/) \> Performance > Clicks. It may take between 4 to 6 hours for data to refresh, but you should see at least 1 click if you navigated to your tracked affiliate link.
