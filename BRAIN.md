# Unique Stays USA

SEO-driven content site for unique short-term rental stays in the US. Affiliate-based revenue model. Built with Next.js + content pipeline.

## Current Phase: Post-Launch → Grow
Validating whether organic traffic converts to affiliate revenue.

## Stack
- Next.js (apps/web), content in `content/`, data in `data/`
- Keyword research + content calendar: `KEYWORD_RESEARCH_AND_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`

## Current Status
- Site is live
- Building content pipeline to drive organic traffic
- Affiliate model in place; testing conversion

## Key Decisions Made
- Affiliate over transactional (no inventory risk)
- Content-first SEO strategy vs paid traffic
- Next.js for flexibility over Webflow/no-code

## Blocking Questions
- Is organic traffic growing?
- Are affiliate clicks converting to bookings/revenue?
- Which content categories drive highest engagement?

## Active Work
- Content production (unique stays by region/category)
- SEO optimization
- Keyword targeting per calendar
