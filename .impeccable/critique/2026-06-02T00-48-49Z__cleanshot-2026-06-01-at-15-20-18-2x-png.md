#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

This is not AI slop. The design has a specific point of view: warm editorial travel, paper edges, terracotta, and display numerals. The issue is not taste. The issue is product ergonomics.

The module currently says: "Here is a beautiful booking card." It should say: "You have enough confidence to tap."

Deterministic scan was unavailable because the bundled detector entrypoint is missing. Browser overlays were skipped because the target is a static screenshot.

#### Overall Impression

The ingredients are right, but the grouping is wrong. Price, reviews, and CTA are the same decision. They should live together. Location, beds, baths, and sleeps are context. They should be lighter and faster to scan.

#### Recommended Mobile Structure

1. Top micro-summary: `Cortez, Colorado · 2 beds · 1 bath · Sleeps 4`
2. Booking block: left `From $422 / night`, underneath `4.98 star · 372 reviews`
3. CTA: `Book on Airbnb` as the dominant action
4. Microcopy: `Final price shown on Airbnb`
5. Sticky bottom bar after scroll: `$422 · 4.98 star` plus `Book`

#### Priority Issues

**[P1] Price, reviews, and CTA are split across two cards**

**Why it matters**: The user's booking decision is made from three signals: cost, trust, and action. Separating them makes the module feel slower than it needs to be.

**Fix**: Put price, rating, review count, Airbnb handoff, and CTA into one compact booking unit.

**Suggested command**: `distill`

**[P2] Metadata is over-labeled for mobile**

**Why it matters**: The uppercase labels create visual noise. On mobile, users scan values first.

**Fix**: Collapse into a sentence or 2-row chip grid. Keep semantic labels for accessibility, but visually lead with values.

**Suggested command**: `adapt`

**[P2] The CTA feels like a poster button, not a mobile control**

**Why it matters**: The wide tracking and huge horizontal block look polished, but slightly less tappable and native.

**Fix**: Reduce letter spacing, keep strong color, and use a height around 56px on mobile. Preserve the external arrow.

**Suggested command**: `typeset`

**[P3] The price should not look final**

**Why it matters**: Affiliate rental pricing is slippery. The UI should prevent disappointment after clickthrough.

**Fix**: Add quiet microcopy: `Final price shown on Airbnb` or `Before fees and taxes`.

**Suggested command**: `clarify`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Casey, distracted mobile user**: The CTA is not persistent. Once Casey scrolls, the action is gone.

**Jordan, first-timer**: The price looks authoritative but may not be final. Jordan needs one sentence of expectation-setting.

**Sam, accessibility-dependent user**: Rating needs accessible text, not just colored stars.

#### Questions to Consider

- What should remain visible after the user scrolls past this module?
- Is the rating a separate feature, or just evidence for the booking decision?
- Would the page feel more confident if the metadata became one readable sentence?
